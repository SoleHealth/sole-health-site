GET TO KNOW THE SERVICES

What’s the difference?

What is a Foot Zone?

Foot Zoning is a focused, hands-on session centered on the feet. Gemstones, along with specific hand movements and acupressure techniques, are used to work with points on the feet that correspond with the whole body. This whole-body approach offers time to slow down, relax, and experience a sense of balance. Many people describe it as a reset for the body.

What is Zone Reflex?

Zone Reflex is a broader hands-on session that works with the body’s systems through specific reflex points in several areas of the body, including the lower legs and feet, forearms and hands, and the neck, shoulders, head and face. Using primarily the hands, gentle and intentional pressure is applied throughout the session. This full-body approach encourages deep relaxation and offers a chance for the nervous system to settle. Many people experience it as a full-body reset.
