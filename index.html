A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before you book.

What is Foot Zoning?+

Foot Zoning at Sole to Soul Health is a relaxing, hands-on wellness experience focused on the feet. Sessions are available for 60 minutes ($50) or 75 minutes ($75).

What is Zone Reflex?+

Zone Reflex is a specialized session using the Zone Reflex method. Choose a 60-minute session ($60) or a 90-minute session ($90).

Who provides the sessions?+

Jill Duncan is a certified Foot Zone practitioner and Master Certified Christ-centered Life Coach. Sole to Soul Health is also trained and certified through Wellness Life Zone in Foot Zoning and Zone Reflex.

What comforts are included?+

Enjoy a comfortable zero-gravity chair, warm soothing towels, and a relaxing eye mask. An optional weighted blanket is available.

Does this website take payment?+

No. This is an informational website. Booking links open the appointment system; payment availability depends on how that booking service is configured.
