A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Meet Jill.

I’ve always enjoyed connecting with people and creating space for meaningful conversation, reflection, and personal growth. My journey with foot zoning began through my own search for ways to strengthen and support my health. Along the way, I discovered a modality I truly love, and I’m grateful to share it with others, especially those who are experiencing foot zoning for the first time.

I also hold a Master Certification in Life Coaching, which has deepened my ability to listen with care and meet each person where they are. Some clients enjoy talking and connecting during a session, while others prefer a peaceful, quiet experience. Both are welcome here. My hope is that every visit offers a little space to pause, reconnect, and leave feeling restored.
